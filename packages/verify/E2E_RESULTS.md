# Cross-chain verification (Oct 3, 2026 — hardened verifier)

`receptum-verify` returns **VERIFIED** only when nothing fails, the payment itself is confirmed on its rail, _and_ (since account bindings, SPEC §4.1) the seller is proven to control the payee; **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** when only anchors could be checked or no binding is present; **NOT VERIFIED** otherwise.

**Account bindings (re-run 2026-10-03).** The x402 receipt was re-issued with a binding and verifies in full including L2.5; the XRPL escrow A and Arc escrow A receipts verify in full with bindings attached afterwards (`examples/xrpl-testnet-escrow-a.json`, `examples/arc-testnet-escrow-a.json`) — XRPL after the switch to chronological delivery derivation (first seller memo after `EscrowCreate`). Without a binding the same receipts are `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`, or `VERIFIED` with `--allow-unbound`. See [examples/E2E_RESULTS.md](../../examples/E2E_RESULTS.md).
